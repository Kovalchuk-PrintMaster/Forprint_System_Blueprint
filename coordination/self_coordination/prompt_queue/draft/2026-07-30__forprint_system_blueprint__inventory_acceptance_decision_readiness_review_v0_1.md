# Inventory Acceptance Decision Readiness Review

## Objective

Confirm that the complete acceptance package is decision-ready before the merge gate.

## Required outputs

- Final acceptance readiness matrix.
- Remaining blockers and exceptions.
- Acceptance authority handoff.
- Merge gate release decision.

## Completion gate

- Evidence is complete and traceable.
- Unknowns remain explicit.
- External rollout remains gated.
