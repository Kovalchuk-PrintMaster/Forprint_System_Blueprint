# Inventory Operational Handoff Readiness

## Objective

Confirm ownership, monitoring and constraints before the final operational readiness review.

## Required outputs

- Operational handoff matrix.
- Ownership and monitoring assignments.
- Remaining post-acceptance constraints.
- Operational readiness release decision.

## Completion gate

- Evidence remains complete and traceable.
- Candidate snapshots remain non-accepted.
- Explicit deferrals remain visible.
- External rollout remains gated.
