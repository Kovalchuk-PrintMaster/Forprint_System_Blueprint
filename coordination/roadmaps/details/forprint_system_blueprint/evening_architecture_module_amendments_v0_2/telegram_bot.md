# telegram_bot — Evening Architecture Amendment v0.2

Status: Recovery-first mature-state planning input.

Telegram is the human communication orchestrator across customers, suppliers, carriers/taxi, service contacts and other approved external participants. Domain modules retain business semantics.

Support controlled menu/form flows for sensitive/financial interactions and natural conversational mode for ordinary communication. Personalization never expands permissions.

Before dispatching a task, collect the target module's required input set where practical.

Preserve traceable conversation episodes linked to participants and business context; one physical chat may contain multiple topics.

Actively close communication loops such as delivery notification, ETA/cost, waiting warnings, receipt confirmation and escalation; future authorized voice/call adapters may support urgent contact.

Because Telegram is one of the oldest repositories, perform deep capability/instruction archaeology before the next portfolio and classify historical cross-domain content against current owners.

Accidental personal messages, pocket/T9 noise, suspicious extraction attempts and unsupported input must be classified before expensive AI/search/downstream work.
