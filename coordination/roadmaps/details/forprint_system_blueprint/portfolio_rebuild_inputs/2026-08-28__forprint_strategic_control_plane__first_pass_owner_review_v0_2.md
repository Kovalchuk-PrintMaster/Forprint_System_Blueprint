## AGREED_WITH_OWNER

Strategic Control Plane is an evidence-backed strategic intelligence layer with two reciprocal modes.

1. Given current architecture, tools, roadmap and target, assess whether the chosen path is realistic,
competitive and likely to reach the goal.
2. Given a strategic objective, research alternative approaches/technologies and recommend plausible paths
with trade-offs.

It may use broad research, model debate/adversarial analysis and human/expert input where justified.

## Working boundary

It is not an oracle and does not automatically override Blueprint/operator decisions. External consensus,
AI debate and market practice are evidence, not authority.

Inspector asks whether execution follows the agreed direction. Strategic Control Plane asks whether the
direction itself still makes sense.
