## Owner revision-1 direction — 2026-08-27

This target-state seed is refined by an inventory-first rule: do not produce the final execution
roadmap until deep structural + semantic inventory maps implemented/reusable/obsolete/partial
functionality to the target state.

Agreed target behavior includes:

- central decision engine for producibility, price, ETA, materials/operations/equipment and
  structured order/job specification;
- dynamic pricing and availability reacting to material, quantity, technology, waste, urgency,
  currency, shortage, staff/equipment state, congestion and temporary commercial policy;
- explicit operational states such as `AVAILABLE`, `CONSTRAINED`, `TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`,
  `HIDDEN`, `MANUAL_QUOTE`;
- Library remains canonical semantic truth; Calculator consumes it;
- production queue/scheduling remains in Calculator for now because ETA depends on it continuously;
  extraction is reconsidered only if scheduling becomes a large independent domain;
- planned material need (including technical waste) remains distinct from physical actual
  consumption and accounting posting;
- close bidirectional Calculator/Prepress interaction for constructors, previews and readiness;
- Website/Telegram/Mobile share Calculator contracts rather than duplicating formulas;
- normal mode is automatic, with human intervention concentrated on abnormal/ambiguous conditions;
- stabilization metrics are class-specific rather than one global tolerance.

Open boundaries remain visible: exact write-off ownership, long-term scheduling ownership,
administrative override UX/security and product-class-specific acceptance metrics.

## Reference services and constructor direction — owner addendum 2026-08-27

Status: `AGREED_WITH_OWNER` for direction and reference use; benchmark priority remains provisional.

### Calculator/service references

Current reference set for studying customer-facing calculator and ordering functionality:

- `https://sborka.ua/` — current primary working reference for the Calculator direction, but not a
  final authority, not an architectural ceiling, and its priority may change after deeper analysis;
- `https://www.fastprint.pro/` — additional calculator/service reference;
- `https://wolf.ua/` — additional calculator/service reference.

These services are examples to analyze for useful behavior, product configuration patterns,
quote/order flow, commercial controls and customer UX. They are not specifications to copy.

### Product constructor references

Initial examples supplied for studying constructor UX and interaction patterns:

- `https://shop.foxminded.ua/en/constructor`
- `https://vizitka.com/uk/creator/universal?productId=5425&productComponentId=11893`

The target is not one isolated constructor. Calculator/Prepress should support a family of
product-specific constructors where customer value justifies them, for example business cards,
apparel, mugs/cups, thermoses, wall art/canvas and other frequently customized products.

The preferred long-term direction is one shared constructor platform/interaction architecture
with reusable primitives and product-specific templates, constraints, print areas, previews and
validation rules, rather than unrelated one-off constructor implementations.

### Future research task — constructor demand prioritization

Before fixing the constructor implementation sequence, perform a separate evidence-based market
and user-demand analysis:

1. identify which personalized-print product constructors users most often search for, use or
   expect from print/e-commerce services;
2. compare demand, conversion value, implementation complexity and production readiness;
3. form a ranked constructor backlog;
4. choose an initial common interaction/design architecture;
5. implement product families iteratively and measure actual usage.

Until that research is complete, the listed product categories are planning examples, not a final
priority order.
