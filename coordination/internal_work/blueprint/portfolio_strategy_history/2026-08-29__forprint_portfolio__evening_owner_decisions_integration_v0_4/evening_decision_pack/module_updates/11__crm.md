# CRM — evening delta

CRM should not physically own a private long-term copy of customer dialogue history.

Persistent customer/conversation data belongs in central managed persistence.

CRM is the semantic/business interface for:
- customer relationship;
- profile;
- contacts;
- supplier/customer reliability history;
- preferences;
- relationship notes;
- communication-oriented views.

Example:
CRM may know a supplier is preferred, alternative or unreliable due to repeated missed dispatch promises.

Active shortage/replenishment workflow belongs to Operations Control Registry.
