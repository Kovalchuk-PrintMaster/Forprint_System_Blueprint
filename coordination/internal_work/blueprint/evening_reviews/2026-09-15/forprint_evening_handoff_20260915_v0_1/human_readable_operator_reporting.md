# Human-Readable Operator Reporting

When the operator asks where we are on the roadmap, answer in plain language.

Example:
- History/recovery foundation is finished.
- We are implementing reusable AI execution profiles.
- Next we formalize governed execution procedures.
- Then we build task/handoff packaging and connect it to Dispatcher.
- Then we run the first controlled Blueprint worker pilot.

Machine IDs may be appended for traceability but must not replace meaning.
