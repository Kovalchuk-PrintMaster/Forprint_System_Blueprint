### ANOM-BP3108-0004 — semantic_document_blind_spot (critical)
Machine inventory alone is insufficient because many Blueprint artifacts encode architecture/interaction meaning requiring semantic review and reconciliation.

### ANOM-BP3108-0005 — make_and_policy_enforcement_gap (high)
Owner identifies Makefile integration and policy-to-code enforcement as gray zones; exact technical gaps are not fully resolved in preserved conversation.

### ANOM-BP3108-0006 — derived_index_without_source_reconciliation_risk (high)
If generated knowledge/index views are rebuilt before structural/semantic cleanup, contradictions and duplicate authority may simply be copied into a cleaner-looking index.

### ANOM-BP3108-0007 — synthetic_vs_accepted_confusion (critical)
Synthetic future roadmap is useful, but if status/color/provenance is lost it can be mistaken for approved architecture.

### ANOM-BP3108-0008 — legacy_file_semantics_entanglement (high)
Historical print-shop filenames encode operational instructions, customer/date conventions and material/device semantics in free-form names; migration must preserve retrieval while normalizing.

### ANOM-BP3108-0009 — semantic_auditor_authority_overreach_risk (critical)
An LLM semantic auditor could drift from finding inconsistencies into inventing/accepting domain rules; proposed Inspector boundary explicitly prevents this.

### ANOM-BP3108-0010 — contract_registry_gateway_boundary_risk (high)
Contract definition/lifecycle and runtime validation/routing are easy to conflate; current implementation must preserve the separated responsibilities.
