# Strategic Vector lifecycle

Persistent coordination mechanism; finite strategic epochs.

Current epoch:
1. finish control foundation;
2. complete Dispatcher;
3. launch one bounded internal Blueprint worker;
4. stabilize;
5. audit/pilot Logistics;
6. scale module workers;
7. form balanced multi-module waves;
8. reach coordinated portfolio operation.

At that boundary:
- close/sunset the current vector version;
- reassess actual state;
- publish the next strategic vector.
